## Proven dead ends — do not reattempt without new evidence

- The old PSMCT16 “vertical bands” compose experiment was a net regression; those bands are 5-bit quantization. It is distinct from the completed FBW/page-placement composer and should not be revived as its next step.
- PSM-aware upload-cache changes were MAE-neutral; the VRAM-stomping hypothesis was disproven.
- The 16:9 image shift is PCSX2 letterboxing, not an NTSC offset bug.
- The 0290 framebuffer scramble is genuine game-side aliasing.
- The forced-magenta-palette experiment and full PCRTC refactor were superseded: the shadow renders correctly and the magenta meter was float32 Z-interpolation overshoot.
